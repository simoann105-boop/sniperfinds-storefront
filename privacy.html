Privacy Policy

How SniperFinds handles waitlist and storefront data.

Information we collect

When you join the waitlist, we collect the information you submit, such as email address, country, purchase intent, product interest, and the problem you want solved.

We may also collect basic page analytics such as page views and button clicks to understand whether the offer is useful.

How we use information

We use waitlist information to validate product demand, improve the offer, contact interested customers, and decide whether to open checkout.

We do not sell personal information. If we use service providers for forms, analytics, email, or checkout, they process data only to help operate the storefront.

Your choices

You can request deletion or correction of your waitlist information by contacting support@sniperfinds.example.

If you receive an email from us, you can unsubscribe or ask us to stop contacting you.
