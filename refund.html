Refund Policy

How refunds and order issues are handled when checkout is open.

Current validation mode

The storefront is currently built for waitlist and demand validation. If checkout is not open, no purchase has been completed and no refund is needed.

When checkout opens, the product page and checkout flow should clearly show price, shipping expectations, and support contact before payment.

Refund window

For paid orders, customers may contact us within 14 days of delivery if the product arrives damaged, defective, materially different from the product page, or unusable.

We may request an order number, photos, or a short description so we can resolve the issue quickly.

Shipping and supplier issues

If a supplier cannot fulfill an order within the stated shipping estimate, we will offer a reasonable resolution such as cancellation, replacement, or refund.

Contact support@sniperfinds.example for order support before initiating a payment dispute whenever possible.
