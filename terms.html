Rules for using the SniperFinds storefront.

Use of the storefront

SniperFinds is a product validation storefront. Product availability, pricing, and checkout status may change as we test demand and supplier readiness.

Do not misuse the website, submit false information, interfere with its operation, or attempt unauthorized access.

Product information

We aim to keep product descriptions, pricing, and availability accurate, but early validation pages may change before checkout opens.

Images marked as illustrative should be replaced with owned, supplier-approved, or self-recorded media before scaling paid traffic.

Orders

If checkout opens, orders are subject to successful payment, supplier availability, shipping destination support, and fraud checks.

We may cancel or refund an order if fulfillment becomes unavailable or if an error affects pricing or availability.
